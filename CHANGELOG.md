# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-06

First version.

- 21 pilots: Tommy and the 20 dinosaurs of Tommy's Dino Zoo, redrawn with
  slimmer, sportier proportions and seated in their karts.
- 12 tracks in 3 cups (Fern, Amber, Volcano) and 4 battle arenas.
- Grand Prix, free race, time trial with ghost, balloon battle.
- Split screen for up to 4 players (gamepads or a shared keyboard).
- Arcade driving with drift turbos, ramps, tricks, boost pads and a ptera
  rescue; race and battle AI.
- Surprise-egg items, friendly and attack ones.
- Garage: coins buy kart bodies, wheels and colours (looks only; every pilot
  and track is free).
- Grown-ups' page: session time limit, items, speed, difficulty, rivals that
  wait, laps, bumping, battle on/off, coins, per-player assists, PIN.
- Spanish and English; synthesised music and sounds.
- macOS and Windows builds from GitHub Actions.
